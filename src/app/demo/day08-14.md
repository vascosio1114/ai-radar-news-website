### Day 8: AI 不會搶你工作，但會用 AI 的那個人會 (職場鞭屍體)

**[中文標題]**
你每天準時打卡，他用 AI 把你的工作外包給演算法。Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei 直言，五年內 50% 的基層知識工作將消失——不是因為 AI 搶飯碗，而是因為有人學會了讓 AI 替他上班。AI Index Report 2026 指出，人形機器人在模擬環境準確率 89.4%，真實世界僅 12%，藍領體力勞動短期內相對安全；但的白領輸入資料、產出報告的岗位，正在被即時消滅。AI 職缺今年一月到二月增長 12 倍，平均月薪 8,400 美金，比新經濟平均高出 26%。你不會被 AI 取代，你會被那個命令 AI 做完所有事的人取代。現在開始學，還來得及。

**[English Title]**
You Won't Lose Your Job to AI — You'll Lose It to Someone Who Uses AI

Your colleague clocks in at 9 AM sharp. Meanwhile, AI handles her entire workload before her first coffee breaks. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei just dropped a warning: 50% of entry-level knowledge jobs could vanish within 1-5 years — not because AI stole them, but because someone learned to make AI do the work instead. The AI Index Report 2026 reveals a stunning sim-to-real gap: humanoid robots score 89.4% in simulation but only 12% in real-world tasks, meaning blue-collar physical jobs are temporarily safe. But knowledge workers processing data and generating reports? They're being wiped out right now. AI job postings surged ~12x year-over-year in early 2026, with average salaries hitting ~$8,400 USD/month — 26% above the new economy average. The brutal truth: you won't be replaced by AI. You'll be replaced by the person who figured out how to make AI do their job. The clock is ticking. Start learning now.
